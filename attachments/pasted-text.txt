1. Project identity

QatraTherm is an integrated solar-powered water treatment and energy valorization concept, based on a conceptual case study in Tendla, El Meghaïer, Algeria.

The installation combines:

- Groundwater extraction and pretreatment.
- Photovoltaic-thermal (PV/T) collectors.
- Buffer storage.
- Reverse osmosis desalination.
- Potable water treatment and storage.
- Concentrated solar thermal collectors (CSP).
- Steam generation and micro-turbine electricity generation.
- Separate brine management through lined solar evaporation basins.
- Intelligent process supervision and control.

3. Most important redesign: intelligent sliding panels

The current technical panels obstruct the 3D model. This must be fixed.

Implement a modern panel interaction system:

A. Collapsible navigation panel

- A compact navigation control remains accessible.
- Clicking it opens a panel from the left.
- Clicking again closes it completely.
- The 3D model becomes fully visible when the panel is closed.

B. Equipment inspection panel

- When a user clicks a 3D component, open its technical information in a panel sliding from the right.
- Include equipment name, function, inputs, outputs, sensors and technical relationships.
- Add an obvious close button.
- Allow the user to close the panel and return to the unobstructed 3D view.
- Do not permanently cover the installation.

C. SCADA panel

- Open and close independently.
- Display process measurements, instrumentation and control information.
- Use a compact dashboard layout.
- Clearly distinguish illustrative or simulated values from real measurements.

D. Interaction requirements

- Smooth slide-in and slide-out transitions.
- Panels must not unexpectedly cover the central 3D model.
- Allow only the necessary panel to be open at a time.
- Provide keyboard Escape support.
- Preserve the user's camera position when opening or closing panels.
- Keep controls accessible without cluttering the scene.

The default experience should show the 3D installation with minimal interface elements.
